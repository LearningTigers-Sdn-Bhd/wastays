# WAStays design rules

Portal rules apply to Admin, Hotel, and Corporate screens. Guest, Concierge,
public, and marketing pages use GuestUI; their contracts are in
`docs/guest-concierge/`. Generated PDFs use their own rule file.

Read only the rule files relevant to the change. The source-of-truth rule applies
across the contract. Historical plans do not override these rules or current code.
